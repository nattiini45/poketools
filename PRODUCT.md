# PRODUCT

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

PLC — a self-hostable tool for competitive Pokémon communities (German and English supported). Four personas share one platform:

- **Players / trainers** — community members who sign in with Discord, register for leagues and tournaments, draft teams, submit and confirm match results, and manage their teams and profiles.
- **Admins** — community organizers who create leagues, tournaments, and rulesets, generate rounds, resolve disputes, and manage the player roster.
- **Creators / casters** — produce overlays for OBS using the broadcast surfaces (no NavBar, spoiler-controlled).
- **Anonymous spectators** — browse tournaments, leagues, standings, replays, wiki, and broadcast overlays without login.

## Product Purpose

A self-hostable competitive Pokémon league and tournament platform. League/season surfaces own *aliveness* — the season made visible, as living spectacle; dashboards own task dispatch — the player's actionable queue. The platform runs bespoke formats (notably Evolution Point Buy / "Evolution Liga") alongside standard singles, doubles, and draft formats, from season creation through drafting, matchdays, replay parsing, stats, and crowning.

## Positioning

The league made visible — a season as living spectacle, not a spreadsheet.

## Brand Personality

Nintendo-grade craft and warmth. Franchise-true: built on the Pokémon world's own materials, colors, and iconography, never ironic distance from them. Confident, playful, precise — playful in voice, precise in data.

## Anti-references

- **Too dark / too edgy** — crypto-esports neon menace; LED-in-a-void aesthetics.
- **Too childish** — clip-art fansite energy; craft must feel Nintendo-grade, not toylike.
- **Too slow / too heavy** — stutter, delayed time-to-content, or battery drain; spectacle may never tax the hardware.
- **Spectacle eats function** — standings, fixtures, and actions must always be findable within seconds; decoration never outranks information.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA. Reduced-motion completeness — every motion-enhanced experience has a fully functional reduced-motion path. Full keyboard operability across all surfaces, including overlays and draft interactions.
