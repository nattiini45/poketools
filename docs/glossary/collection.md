# Collection

The optional collecting layer around the leagues: trainers catch Pokémon, build Careers with them and earn Ribbons and Trainer titles. It never changes anything in a league.

## Language

**Collection**:
The Pokémon a trainer has caught, at most one per species; only fully evolved and single-stage species can be caught.
_Avoid_: Box, inventory

**Pokédex**:
A trainer's view of every catchable species, showing which are in their Collection.
_Avoid_: Dex list, collection page

**Catch**:
Getting one new species into the Collection by opening a ball; it always succeeds.
_Avoid_: Pull, roll, gacha, draw

**Ball charge**:
One saved-up ball a trainer can open; charges accrue every 12 hours, up to three.
_Avoid_: Energy, ticket, token

**Starter**:
The one catchable species a trainer picks freely as their first Catch.
_Avoid_: First pick, gift

**Partner**:
A Pokémon in a trainer's Collection, which they own and can nickname, show and trade.
_Avoid_: Owned mon

**Loaner**:
An evolution line a trainer uses in a league without owning its final species; it plays like any other Pokémon.
_Avoid_: Borrowed Pokémon, rental

**Look**:
One way a Partner can appear: its default look, its Shiny look or a Cosmetic form; never a separate Pokémon.
_Avoid_: Skin, variant

**Shiny look**:
The alternate colouring a Partner has when it was caught shiny (1 in 2048); it cannot be unlocked later.
_Avoid_: Shiny variant

**Cosmetic form**:
A Look unlocked by battling with a Partner: one for every fifth battle while you own it.
_Avoid_: Costume

**Career**:
Everything earned with a Pokémon, in two parts: Trainer stats and Ribbons.
_Avoid_: History, record

**Trainer stats**:
A trainer's battles, wins and kills with one evolution line, Loaner battles included; they stay with the trainer and never move in a Trade.
_Avoid_: Pokémon stats

**Ribbon**:
An award that belongs to one Partner, travels with it in a Trade and records which trainer earned it; each Ribbon is earned once per Partner.
_Avoid_: Badge, medal, achievement

**Held ribbons**:
Ribbons a trainer earned with a Loaner, kept per line until they move onto the first Partner of that species the trainer catches or receives.
_Avoid_: Pending ribbons

**Partner ribbon**:
A Ribbon only a Partner can earn, for feats after it was caught.
_Avoid_: Bond ribbon

**Owner history**:
The ordered list of trainers who held a Partner, from its original trainer to its current handler.
_Avoid_: Provenance

**Trade**:
Two trainers swapping one Partner each, both agreeing and neither already owning the other's species.
_Avoid_: Exchange, gift

**Trainer title**:
A public honour a trainer earns by winning with one evolution line on the battle team, for example "Glurak-Fan" (5), "-Experte" (20), "-Meister" (50), "-Legende" (100); it is named after the line's drafted final species.
_Avoid_: Rank, badge

**Favourite team**:
Up to six Partners a trainer shows on their profile.
_Avoid_: Showcase, signature team (Signature Pokémon are a separate feature)

**Chronicle**:
The public feed of notable events on the dashboard: rare-or-better Catches, shinies, new Trainer titles, rare Ribbons and Trades.
_Avoid_: Activity feed, timeline

**Wish ticket**:
A reward that lets a trainer pick one missing species (later idea).
_Avoid_: Voucher
