# In a Career, trainer stats belong to the trainer and ribbons belong to the Pokémon

A Career mixes two things a trainer earns with a Pokémon, and they follow different owners. Trainer stats and Trainer titles belong to the trainer per evolution line — battles, wins and kills accumulated with that line, Loaner battles included — and never move in a Trade: the trainer keeps what they did. A Ribbon belongs to the individual caught Pokémon: it travels with that Partner in a Trade, records which trainer earned it, and each Ribbon is earned once per Pokémon, so a traded Partner cannot farm the same Ribbon twice.

## Consequences

- A Trade moves a Partner's Ribbons and Owner history, and leaves the two trainers' Trainer stats untouched.
- Ribbons earned with a Loaner are held per line until a Partner of that species arrives, then move onto it (the [Collection glossary](../glossary/collection.md), "Held ribbons").
