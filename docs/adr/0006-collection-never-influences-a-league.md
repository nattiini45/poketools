# The collection system never influences a league

Collecting sits around the leagues — a trainer catches Pokémon, builds Careers with them and earns Ribbons and Trainer titles — and the obvious temptation is to make collecting pay off in competition. We decided it never does: nothing a trainer catches or earns gives them a stronger Pokémon, draft points or any other advantage in a league. The collection is for collecting, achievements and a story across leagues.

## Considered options

- Let a Collection grant a small edge (a pick, a re-roll, a point). Rejected: leagues would be decided partly by a side activity, and a trainer who ignores collecting would be behind through no fault of their drafting or play.
- Reward collecting only inside its own surfaces. That is the decision, not an alternative.

## Consequences

- A league never reads from the collection: no Collection lookup in standings, drafting, results or rulesets.
- A Loaner is the only crossing, and it grants nothing: a line a trainer does not own plays like any other Pokémon (see the [Collection glossary](../glossary/collection.md)).
