## Material loaded at runtime (not redistributed)

The app fetches some material from third-party hosts at runtime. None of it is stored in this repository; it stays under its owners' terms, and a deployment that uses these features relies on those hosts.

- Pokémon HOME renders from [pokemondb.net](https://pokemondb.net) (`img.pokemondb.net`): the `home` sprite style and the fallback of every other style.
- Animated and pixel Pokémon sprites from [Pokémon Showdown](https://pokemonshowdown.com) (`play.pokemonshowdown.com`): the `animated` and `pixel` sprite styles.
- Trainer sprites and the item-icon sheet from [Pokémon Showdown](https://pokemonshowdown.com) (`play.pokemonshowdown.com`).
- Usage statistics from Smogon, through the [pkmn](https://github.com/pkmn) data mirror (`data.pkmn.cc`).
- Battle replays from Pokémon Showdown, when a user submits a replay URL.

The `@pkmn/*` npm packages (game data and replay parsing) are dependencies under their own licences.
